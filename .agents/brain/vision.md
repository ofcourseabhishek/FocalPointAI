# Vision

> Snapgrade is an AI photography learning platform that teaches photographers why an image works, not just whether it scores well.

## Target Users
- Photographers looking to improve their craft
- Visual artists who want to understand the "why" behind an image's impact

## Philosophy
- Teach, don't just judge
- Emphasize reasoning and composition over arbitrary scores
- Nurture the creative director mindset

## What Snapgrade should never become
- A generic AI image rater
- A platform that discourages artistic expression in favor of "perfect" technical scores
